# Product

## Register

product

## Users

QuantX serves individual investors and semi-professional researchers who want the discipline of quantitative research without first learning every professional configuration surface. The simplified workspace is for users who need one clear path from a strategy idea to a trustworthy observation decision: choose a template, check data, run a backtest, understand whether the result is credible, then decide whether to observe it in simulation.

Professional users still need dense research tools elsewhere in the product, but the default simplified experience should reduce cognitive load while preserving the checks that keep research honest.

## Product Purpose

QuantX helps users evaluate A-share quantitative strategies with traceable experiments, point-in-time data checks, realistic execution constraints, and simulation workflows. Success means a user can understand where a backtest came from, whether it used data that was actually visible at the time, whether trades could realistically execute under A-share rules, and whether the strategy is safe enough to observe.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, approachable.

The product should feel like a patient research assistant: plain-spoken enough for first-time users, disciplined enough for serious strategy work, and unwilling to hide risk behind attractive returns.

## Anti-references

Do not make the simplified workspace look like a generic blue SaaS dashboard. Avoid screens packed with unexplained metrics, professional jargon without translation, decorative status cards, and result pages that celebrate high returns without showing future-function risk, data freshness, or execution feasibility.

Avoid treating the simplified mode as a toy. It should be easier to use, not less truthful.

## Design Principles

1. One decision per screen: each simplified step should answer one user question before moving on.
2. Trust before excitement: show audit and execution credibility before encouraging observation.
3. Plain labels over internal terms: users see "可信度", "能不能成交", and "为什么不能观察", not implementation names.
4. Progressive depth: keep the main path simple, with details available in drawers or reports.
5. Professional mode stays stable: simplified iterations must not disrupt existing lab, factor, portfolio, or settings workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-operable controls. Reduced motion must be respected. Financial status should not rely on color alone: risk, warning, and pass states need text labels as well as semantic color.
